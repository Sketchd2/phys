Off, the structure is drawn where a load held forever would put it. On, it has mass: it leans into a gust, swings back out of one, and rings after a limb goes. The gust is an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck fluctuation about the mean, which is the standard model of boundary-layer turbulence and the reason the motion is not periodic.
